I develop computer vision and machine learning methods that use color and depth (RGB-D) imaging to detect, track, and assess the maturity of individual crops, supporting robotic harvesting and crop monitoring. My current work focuses on white button mushrooms (Agaricus bisporus).

I am a PhD candidate in Agricultural and Biological Engineering at the University of Florida, based at the Gulf Coast Research and Education Center in Wimauma, Florida, advised by Dr. Daeun Choi. I received an M.S. in Computer Science from UF in 2022 and a B.Tech. in Computer Science from Banasthali Vidyapith in 2021.

Research

Depth-guided detection, segmentation, and tracking

RD-GuideNet uses depth to guide detection, instance segmentation, and temporal tracking of individual mushrooms in dense beds, reaching an F1-score of 0.93 for segmentation and 92.7% tracking consistency.

Sensors 2026, Editor’s Choice

Maturity estimation and time-to-harvest prediction

A two-stage framework that estimates maturity from mushroom geometry, depth, and temporal growth features (MAE of 5.77), then predicts time to harvest. Ongoing work examines how crowding affects growth.

ASABE 2024, 2026

RGB-D time-series dataset of mushroom growth

A longitudinal RGB-D dataset of white button mushroom growth with instance annotations, collected with a linear-rail imaging system and released publicly for segmentation research.

Data in Brief 2026, Mendeley Data
